# Marketing Format Purpose

## Scope

This note distinguishes the two requested Tuckmark marketing assets by their
published consumption context. It does not treat either asset as a product UI
capture. The product facts used below come from the repository: Tuckmark is an
agent-native label-printing workspace with printer discovery, template
rendering, structured batch preview, direct bitmap printing, and CLI, MCP, HTTP,
and Web surfaces ([README.md](../../README.md)).

## 4:5 Product Poster

### Sourced facts

- Meta documents `4:5` as a supported Instagram Stream image aspect ratio in
  its [Instagram media requirements](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/instagram/ads-api/reference/media-requirements).
- Meta's [Facebook Feed image guide](https://www.facebook.com/business/ads-guide/update/image)
  recommends `4:5` at `1440 x 1800` and describes the placement as showing a
  product, service, or brand.
- Meta's [Instagram image-ad practices](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/109732209367483)
  recommends concept-driven images that tell a business story and use a
  recognisable logo, icon, or colour scheme.

### Design inference for Tuckmark

This is a portrait, feed-first product poster: it must communicate what
Tuckmark makes possible before a viewer reads supporting copy. Use the hierarchy
`Tuckmark -> concise label-printing promise -> one coherent physical or abstract
label-output concept -> a very small number of result-oriented feature cues`.

Suitable factual cues are template-driven labels, structured inputs becoming
batches, and direct physical output. They should appear as printed labels,
label stock, tactile output, or a brand-native abstract transformation rather
than an application screen. Do not include browser windows, dashboards, panels,
cards, tables, toolbars, device frames, or screenshots.

## 2:1 Social Graphic

### Sourced facts

- GitHub's [social media preview documentation](https://docs.github.com/en/repositories/managing-your-repositorys-settings-and-features/customizing-your-repository/customizing-your-repositorys-social-media-preview)
  says this image displays when someone links to a repository and recommends
  `1280 x 640` pixels, which is exactly `2:1`.
- The [Open Graph Protocol](https://ogp.me/#metadata) defines `og:image` as the
  image that represents an object in the graph and `og:title` as its displayed
  title. It does not prescribe a `2:1` ratio or an in-image layout.

### Design inference for Tuckmark

This is a wide, link-share recognition asset rather than a compressed version of
the portrait poster. Its job is immediate association with the repository and
product when seen alongside share metadata. Use the hierarchy
`Tuckmark -> very short category or promise -> one continuous non-UI label-printing
concept`.

It should carry less copy and no feature list. The wide composition can make the
brand and product category legible at a glance, then use a single physical or
abstract label-production motif to substantiate the promise. As with the poster,
do not depict any concrete UI element.

## Constraint Summary

Platform sources establish the placement and technical dimensions, not a
mandatory content hierarchy. The hierarchy above is a design inference grounded
in those contexts and in the repository's verified product contract. It is not a
claim that any platform requires a particular artistic treatment.
